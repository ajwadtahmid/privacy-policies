## Overview

BacklogForge is an independently developed app that helps you track and manage your
video game backlog. It supports importing your Steam library, tracking play status,
logging completion, and viewing statistics about your game collection. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation.

This app does not require a dedicated account and does not collect personal information
such as your name, email address, or any form of identity beyond what is used for
optional Steam integration.

---

## Data Collected

### Crash & Error Reports
Collected via **Sentry** (sentry.io). When the app crashes or encounters an error,
Sentry automatically sends a report containing:
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- A snapshot of app state at the time of the error

Data is transmitted to Sentry via encrypted HTTPS connection. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. It is not sold or shared with any
third party beyond Sentry's infrastructure.

### Steam Integration
If you choose to connect your Steam account, the app uses your Steam ID to fetch your
public game library. Standard server logs may record:
- Your IP address
- Request timestamps
- Steam API requests made on your behalf

Your Steam credentials are never stored by this app. Steam login is handled entirely
through Steam's own authentication flow. This data is not linked to your identity beyond
your Steam ID, not shared with third parties, and server logs are deleted on a rolling
30-day basis.

### Local App Data
Your game library, play status, notes, ratings, and settings are stored **locally on
your device only** using a local database. This data is never transmitted anywhere and
remains fully under your control.
