## Overview

Heisenbudget is an independently developed personal expense and income tracker. It
works completely offline: it does not require an account, does not connect to the
internet, and does not collect personal information of any kind. Everything you enter
stays on your phone. The app has no server of its own. The one way your data can reach
Google or Apple is your phone's own backup, if you have it switched on (see **Phone
Backups** below).

Heisenbudget is not affiliated with or endorsed by Breaking Bad, Better Call Saul, or their owners.

## Data Collected

### None.

Heisenbudget does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There is no backend server,
no analytics platform, no advertising, no crash reporting service, and no third-party
SDK that sends data anywhere.

## What Stays on Your Device

All app data is stored locally inside the app's private storage on your phone:

| Data | Where it lives | When it's deleted |
|---|---|---|
| Transactions (amount, income or expense, date, category, optional title and note) | App database on your device | When you delete the transaction, use **Erase everything**, or delete the app |
| Categories (names and icons) | App database on your device | When you use **Erase everything** (custom ones can also be archived), or delete the app |
| Repeating transactions, favorites, and budget limits | App database on your device | When you delete them, use **Erase everything**, or delete the app |
| Settings (currency, theme, accent color, text font, wording style, opening balance, week and budget-month settings, whether your first favorite gets a home-screen shortcut, the category you used last, whether you have finished the welcome screen, the version of the data format, and the date of your last backup) | App preferences on your device | When you use **Erase everything**, or delete the app |
| A safety copy of the database, made only before an update that changes how the app stores data | A file in the app's private storage | After 14 days, when you use **Erase everything**, or when you delete the app |

None of this data is ever transmitted off your device by the app.

## Backup, Restore, and Export

Heisenbudget can create a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet file (CSV) of your data.
These files are created on your device and handed to your phone's own share sheet, where
**you** choose where they go (Files, a cloud drive, email, and so on). The app does not
send them anywhere itself. Once you send a file to another app or service, that app or
service's own privacy policy applies to the copy it receives.

Backup and CSV files are not encrypted: they are readable by whatever app or service you
send them to, so choose where they go with that in mind.

A temporary copy of each file is kept in the app's private cache so the share sheet can
read it. The app deletes it automatically about ten minutes after you share it (the wait
gives the app you chose time to take the file), when you next open the app, and when you
use **Erase everything**.

To restore, you choose a backup file with your phone's file picker. The app can read only
the file you pick. Your phone's file picker may make a temporary copy of it while you
choose; the app removes that copy once it has read the file. If the file you pick is stored
in a cloud drive, your phone's drive app may download it first; that is done by that app,
not by Heisenbudget.

## Phone Backups (Google and iCloud)

Heisenbudget has no server of its own and uses no third-party service: the app never sends
your data anywhere. But your phone may have its own backup switched on, and that backup can
copy the app's data to your Google or Apple account without Heisenbudget doing anything. This
is how phones normally treat every app, and it is on by default on many phones.

- **Android:** if Google's automatic backup is on in your phone's settings, Android can
  include Heisenbudget's data (your transactions and settings) in your Google account's
  backup, and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is handled by Google.
- **iPhone and iPad:** if iCloud Backup is on (or you back up to a computer), iOS includes
  Heisenbudget's data in that backup, and restores it onto a new device. This is handled by
  Apple.

What this means for you:

- The copy is stored and protected by Google or Apple under their own terms and privacy
  policies. The developer of Heisenbudget cannot see, read, change, or delete it.
- Heisenbudget has no setting that turns your phone's backup on or off. You can turn it off
  for your phone, or for individual apps where your phone allows it, in your phone's backup
  settings. Without it, the backup file you make yourself (see above) is how you move to a
  new phone.
- **Erase everything** and deleting the app remove the data from your phone. Older copies in a
  Google or Apple backup stay there until that service replaces them with a newer backup, or
  you delete them in your Google or Apple account.
- A backup file you make in the app and send to Google Drive or iCloud Drive yourself is a
  separate thing: it goes only where you choose, and it is not encrypted.

## Crash and Diagnostic Reports

Heisenbudget has no crash reporting of its own and sends nothing when something goes wrong.
If an unexpected error happens, the app may write a technical message to your phone's own
system log, which stays on your phone.

If you have turned on sharing of usage and diagnostics on your phone, Google (Android) or
Apple (iPhone and iPad) may collect crash reports from the operating system and show the
developer the crash details through Google Play Console or App Store Connect. This is done
by the operating system and the store under Google's or Apple's own policies, not by
Heisenbudget, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings. These reports describe the
crash itself (for example the device model, the system version and a technical trace);
Heisenbudget does not attach your transactions or notes to them.
